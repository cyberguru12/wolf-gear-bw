OUR STORY

Built with purpose.
Led by character.

Founder: Calvin Kagiso William

WOLF GEAR BW was founded with a simple idea: clothing can represent more than style. It can represent standards. The brand brings together a bold wolf identity and a code centred on discipline, integrity and transparency.

The vision is to grow WOLF GEAR BW into a recognizable Botswana lifestyle brand while building a community of people who encourage one another to work hard, stay accountable and keep moving forward.
